No account, no personal registration

Beat Procrastination does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal registration information to use it.

Data stored on your device

All of your data — tasks, focus session history, and app settings — is stored locally on your device only. We do not operate a server or backend, and we do not collect, transmit, or have access to this data. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes it.

Advertising

The App is funded by advertising, served through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain technical and advertising identifiers (such as your device's advertising ID) to show and measure ads, and may use this information in accordance with its own privacy practices. You can learn more about how Google uses this data at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where required by applicable law (for example, in the European Economic Area and the UK), the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent before showing personalized ads. You can review or change your choice at any time from the App's Settings screen, where available.

Permissions used

  • Internet / network state — required to request and display ads.
  • Notifications — optional; used only to alert you when a focus session or break ends.
  • Foreground service — used to keep your focus timer running accurately while the screen is off.
  • Vibration — optional; used for the end-of-session alert if you enable it in Settings.

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or location.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
